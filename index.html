Robot Multi-Sensor Module

Stereo + dToF + Radar

A Jetson SDK and GUI for viewing stereo, dToF, and radar streams from the Multi Perception Module.

  • dToF distance and status data
  • Radar point cloud with Doppler and SNR
  • Time-synchronized multi-sensor framesets
  • Prebuilt multi-module GUI viewer

AI ISP Camera SDK

AI Camera + Zero-Copy CUDA

Bring up a MIPI camera on Jetson and connect captured frames to GPU-accelerated imaging workflows.

  • Automated install for driver, DTBO, boot config, and camera service
  • Sensor Direct camera viewer with live gain and exposure control
  • Zero-copy CUDA reference pipeline with latency statistics
  • Prepared for TensorRT and custom AI ISP stages
  • Validated on Jetson AGX Orin with JetPack 6.2
JetPack 6.2 MIPI Sensor CUDA 12 C++

VisionLink RID Camera SDK

RGB + IR + Structured-Light Depth

A Windows C/C++ SDK for controlling the LG Innotek RID camera and acquiring images and 3D point clouds.

  • Five RGB, IR, depth, and dual capture modes
  • Instant mode switching without stream restart
  • BGR24 images and structured-light 3D points
  • Runtime gain, exposure, and illumination control
  • USB authentication and automatic logging
Windows C++17 RGB / IR Depth

UDIR Camera System

Under Display Infrared Camera

A DMS infrared camera concealed behind an automotive display, with Jetson AI restoring image quality degraded by panel transmission.

  • 1280×960 RAW10 fixed-focus IR camera
  • Less visually intrusive behind-display integration
  • TensorRT and CUDA restoration of panel degradation
  • Restored and standard mono viewing modes
  • Face and eye-blink monitoring demo
